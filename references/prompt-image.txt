The image service makes photographs, posters, and entry icons, illustrations and decorative elements in the style the user picked on the visual board. Control icons (navigation, tab bars, menus, buttons, toolbars, tables, forms) and interface screens are drawn as line vectors in HTML, never generated (visual-design.html, 图像生成). Write one prompt file per image under raw/02-visual/prompts/, dry-run first, then generate one at a time.

=== Store, product and environment photo ===
A realistic photograph of [subject: store interior, product, training area, dish] for [brand] in [industry].
Shared style block (identical for every image in the session): bright natural light, clean uncluttered composition, light background, [primary colour] and [accent colour] appearing only as small accents in real objects, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no identifiable faces (people only from behind or as silhouettes).
Framing: [aspect ratio, e.g. 4:3 or 1:1], [shot: wide / medium / close-up], subject centred with even margins so it crops cleanly to the same ratio as the rest of its group.
Avoid: 3D render look, plastic or glossy CGI objects, gradients, glows, neon, heavy filters, dark night scenes unless the user asked for them, collage, device mockups.

=== Poster ===
Create one [format and exact aspect ratio] poster for [brand/product].
Audience and context: [who sees it, placement, viewing distance].
Communication goal: [one business outcome].
Primary headline: [exact copy]. Supporting proposition: [exact copy]. Required details: [exact copy]. Call to action: [copy and QR/link destination].
Composition: one dominant focal point (a real photograph or a flat graphic shape), clear copy hierarchy of at most four levels, generous safe margins of about 5%, strong thumbnail recognition. Leave a protected [size] quiet zone for the QR code and keep the lower [px or percent] clear of platform overlays.
Art direction: light background, flat solid colours from the theme palette, [photography direction], one typeface family. No textures, gradients, 3D objects, glows or glass effects.
Do not render unverified price, address, legal text, logo, testimonial, or QR destination. Do not add tiny microcopy, unrelated promotions, device frames, or fake seals. Do not imitate a named living artist; when a reference is supplied, borrow only the stated structural attribute and replace its brand, copy, imagery and identity.

=== Entry icon, illustration or decorative element ===
One [object, e.g. membership card, gift box, meeting microphone] as a [entry icon | spot illustration | hero element] for [brand] in [industry], used in [placement: entry grid, capability list, empty state, result page, brand card hero].
Shared style block (identical for every icon and illustration of the product; the style the user picked on the visual board): [chosen style, e.g. 3D toy, soft clay, soft 3D, layered flat, hand-drawn line], [primary colour] and [accent colour] from the theme steps as the main colours, soft even light from the upper left, [view, e.g. front three-quarter], one object centred with about 12% padding.
Output: transparent background (PNG); icons square at twice the display size (e.g. 192x192 for a 96 px tile), illustrations at [ratio]. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or identifiable faces; no background scene; no shadow outside the object unless the style block asks for a contact shadow.
Candidates on the visual board: 2–3 styles chosen from the industry's mood and the scene, one sample of the same object per style. Each object gets its own image and reuses it everywhere.
Element sheet (preferred for icons and illustrations): one request for a 4x4 grid of 16 objects in the shared style block, each centred in its own cell with even gutters and nothing crossing cell borders, transparent background; list the 16 objects in reading order. Cut the sheet with scripts/cut_element_sheet.py: it drops faint specks and pieces of neighbouring cells, trims with 6% padding and saves each object as a 240 px WebP.
